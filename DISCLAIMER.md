**Use Halcyon at your own risk.**

manufacturer. It controls keyboards using protocols that were reverse engineered, and much of it has been
tested on only a few keyboards, or not at all.

Sending commands to a keyboard can change its settings, stored keymaps, macros, animations and lighting. It
could, in rare cases, leave a keyboard misbehaving or unusable ("bricked"), and could void its warranty.

- Halcyon is provided "as is", without warranty of any kind, as stated in the [licence](LICENSE).

Keyboard definitions written by other people choose which USB device Halcyon talks to and which commands it
sends. Only use definitions from sources you trust, and review them before use.

If you are not comfortable with these risks, do not use Halcyon with your keyboard. `halcyon --demo` lets you
explore the whole app without sending anything to any device.
