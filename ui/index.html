      Add a keyboard

      Keyboards are small definition files, so you can add yours without touching any code.

      Adding and testing keyboards is at your own risk: a wrong definition could send commands that misconfigure or brick a keyboard, and you are solely responsible for that. See DISCLAIMER.md.

      How it works
      1. Copy a definition from halcyon/devices/, then change its name and USB IDs.
      2. Boards that ship the HFD vendor driver can import their layout: python tools/import_hfd_layout.py KeyboardLayout.xml
      3. Save it in your keyboards folder and press Reload. Share it upstream so everyone gets it.

      Before you start

      Halcyon is independent, community-made software. It is not made or supported by any keyboard maker, and it controls keyboards with reverse-engineered protocols that have been tested on only a few keyboards.

      Sending commands can change your keyboard's settings, keys, macros and animations. In rare cases it could leave a keyboard misbehaving or unusable (bricked), or void its warranty.

      You use Halcyon, and add or test keyboards with it, entirely at your own risk. You are solely responsible for any damage to your keyboard or other devices. The authors and contributors accept no responsibility or liability for it.

      Want to look around first? Start Halcyon with --demo: nothing is sent to any device.
